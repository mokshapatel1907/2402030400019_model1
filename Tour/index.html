Top Tourist Destinations

Discover famous places around the world

North Goa

Goa, India
Open 24 Hours

North Goa is famous for beaches, nightlife and tourism attractions.

Taj Mahal

Agra, India
6:30 AM - 7 PM

One of the seven wonders of the world and symbol of eternal love.

Jaipur

Rajasthan, India
Open 24 Hours

Jaipur is known as the Pink City, famous for forts and palaces.

South Maldives

South Maldives, India
Open 24 Hours
Resort Entry paid 8,000-15,000 INR

The South Maldives (like the Addu Atoll) is famous for its pristine, untouched coral reefs, vibrant marine biodiversity (including manta rays and whale sharks), and tranquil, less-crowded luxury resorts.

Agra Fort

Agra, India
9:30 AM - 5:30 PM (TUE - SUN)
Entry: ₹150

The Agra Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most iconic landmarks in Agra.It is a symbol of the rich history of the Indian subcontinent.

Fatehpur Sikri

Agra, India
6:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Entry: ₹120

Fatehpur Sikri is famous as a well-preserved 16th-century Mughal ghost town. Built by Emperor Akbar, this UNESCO World Heritage Site features a stunning blend of Hindu and Persian architecture

Delhi Akshardham

Delhi, India
10:00 AM - 6:30 PM (TUE - SUN)
Entry: ₹150

Delhi Akshardham is famous for being the world's largest comprehensive Hindu temple. It is renowned for its stunning ancient architecture, intricate stone carvings, sprawling gardens, and a mesmerizing musical fountain show.

Varanasi

Uttar Pradesh, India
6:00 AM - 9:00 PM
Entry: Free

Varanasi is famous as India's spiritual capital. Located on the Ganges, it is one of the world's oldest living cities, renowned for its sacred ghats, nightly Ganga Aarti, and exquisite traditional Banarasi silk.

Andaman Island

Andaman and Nicobar Islands, India
Open 24 Hours
Resort Entry paid 5,000-10,000 INR

The Andaman Islands are famous for their pristine white-sand beaches (like Radhanagar), crystal-clear turquoise waters, and vibrant coral reefs. They are a top destination for scuba diving, water sports, and rich marine biodiversity.

Kathmandu

Kathmandu, Nepal
Open 24 Hours
Entry: ₹570

Kathmandu is world-renowned for its stunning ancient temples, rich cultural heritage, and UNESCO-listed Kathmandu Durbar Square. It serves as Nepal's bustling capital and the primary gateway to the Himalayas.

Tibet

Tibet Autonomous Region, China
Open 24 Hours
Entry: ₹500

Tibet is famous as the "Roof of the World" and the "Land of Snows". It is globally renowned for its breathtaking Himalayan landscapes, ancient Buddhist monasteries, and as the traditional residence of the Dalai Lama.
